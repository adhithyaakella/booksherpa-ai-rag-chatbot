# BookSherpa AI User Guide 📚

Welcome to your personalized AI Tutor for *Natural Language Processing with Transformers*!

## 🚀 Features

### 1. Main Chat 💬
Ask questions freely about the book.
*   **Best for**: Exploring concepts, asking "how" or "why".
*   **Teacher Persona**: The AI will answer and suggest follow-up questions to guide your learning.
*   **Visuals**: If a page has a relevant diagram, it will appear automatically below the answer.

### 2. AI Tutor (Conversational Quiz) 👩‍🏫
A dedicated chat mode where the AI quizzes *you*.
*   **How to Start**: Just type a topic! e.g., *"Chapter 1"* or *"BERT Architecture"*.
*   **Interaction**: The AI will ask one question at a time.
*   **Switching Topics**: Bored? Just type *"Switch to Attention Mechanism"* or *"New quiz on Chapter 3"* to start fresh immediately.

### 3. Chapter Summarizer 📖
Quickly digest entire chapters.
*   **How to Use**: Go to the "Summarizer" tab and enter a Chapter name (e.g., *"Chapter 4"*).
*   **Result**: A high-level summary of the key concepts in that chapter.

### 4. Session Persistence 💾
Never lose your chat history.
*   **Sidebar**: Enter a `User ID` (e.g., your name) in the sidebar profile.
*   **Restore**: Next time you open the app, enter the same name to restore your previous conversation.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**"I don't know" Response**
*   This means the AI couldn't find the answer in the provided text.
*   **Fix**: Try rephrasing your question or asking about a broader topic.

**Images not Loading**
*   Ensure `ingest.py` ran successfully. Images are stored in `frontend/static/images`.
